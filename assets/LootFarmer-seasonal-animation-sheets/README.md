# Seasonal character animation sheets

48 original complete PNG sheets: 28 from character preview 1 and 20 from character preview 2. Each source is included exactly once. These are existing source sheets, not cropped screenshots or generated art.

Each PNG preserves its source bytes, transparency, all animation rows and padding: 216 x 120 pixels, 24 x 24 cells, 9 columns x 5 rows. Some cells are intentionally empty. The v1 dark-outline variant matches the supplied previews; v2 adds a white outline and is intentionally not duplicated here.

Folders: spring, summer, autumn, winter; each contains units and bosses. Full-body costumes/creatures are bosses. Hats, head masks, helmets and partial clothing are units. Thus the sunflower, bee, robot, snowman, reaper and full animal bodies are bosses; pumpkin/TV/space helmets and the calico head-only costume are units. These are organizational assignments, not gameplay changes.

Authored seasonal outfits keep their season; each preview-2 cat stays with its pictured seasonal row. Generic preview-1 characters are assigned by appearance, ready to move if desired. Spring/rain share spring; summer and autumn have separate folders even though they share the supplied environment reference. No map tiles or screenshots are included.

index.csv maps each filename to its exact source and preview position (left to right, top to bottom). Names are descriptive labels, not new approved game identities.
