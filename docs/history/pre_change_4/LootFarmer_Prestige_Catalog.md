# LootFarmer — Prestige Content v1 — Change 3

Approved structure: 11 paths, 33 unique one-use opportunities, 33 unique cards, 11 same-path sets and 6 mixed sets. Names, exact triggers/requirements and small numerical benefits below are delegated authoring, not previously user-selected examples or measured pacing.

Every discovered opportunity tracks without Start/Accept. Donation/submission is the substantive spending action, not an activation requirement. Auto tracking marks readiness when the requirements are met. Discovery triggering action counts if relevant; no retroactive progress before discovery except the approved lifetime offline counters. Online grants/actions only for ordinary paths. Ordered hunt/mission steps count only the current step; unrelated kills do not break these authored routes (they are sequences of objectives, not a consecutive-kills rule). Restricted boss fights begin at first hostile contact and end on death, leash/reset or player death; any manual/automatic consumable use invalidates that encounter only. Discovery from a first boss kill does not retroactively prove a restriction that was not recorded; next encounter qualifies.

Collection submissions keep at least one owned copy and cannot consume equipped last copies. Track online-acquired eligible-copy credits and available physical stock; offline copies cannot fulfill submission requirements. Offering tracks online-earned spending credit, bounded by current currency; offline/mission currency cannot independently satisfy it. Spending is explicit, no automatic confiscation. Trophies are quest flags, no inventory slots; after discovery each listed boss drops its trophy guaranteed on next kill. They are not the permanent final keys and reset with ordinary challenge progress.

## Reachability and pacing

Each path has exactly three opportunities and three cards. No opportunity requires already owning a card in its own path. Spring mastery intentionally requires final-boss access; earlier paths do not. Offline readiness/progress persists across resets. Challenges cannot be advanced by an offline inventory snapshot, currency settlement or offline kill simulation. At least early hunt/masterwork/challenge options must be tuned to become ready near 1–2 hours, not necessarily all of them. Validate every goal against actual reward rates before claiming that target.
