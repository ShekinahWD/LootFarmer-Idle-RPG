# LootFarmer — Change 13 Playbook Compliance Audit

Date: 2026-10-22. Scope: the current game after Change 13. The audit used `docs/Reusable_Game_Development_Playbook.md` as it stands in the repository.

The audit is applicability-based. Each row cites source, resource or test evidence. Rules that do not apply to this game are marked N/A; nothing was built only to satisfy them. "Fixed" means a defect found during this audit and corrected in Change 13. This audit is not a claim that the whole game complies. Desktop and headless checks cannot prove phone layout, colour, audio or performance; those items are listed in the last column and in the Android checklist at the end.

Evidence keys: **P13** = `tests/change_13_probe` (154 checks), **CAP** = `tests/change_13_capture` (desktop renders in `docs/Change_13_Evidence/ui/`), **VAL** = `tools/validate_campaign_source.py` (1,358 checks), **SUITE** = `python tools/run_probes.py` (22 probes).

## Defects found and fixed during the audit

1. Raw currency text: locked camp prices (the reported bug), supply shop buttons, Reroll and its confirmation, the enemy panel's coin and gem drops, Farming Guide rewards, gem entries in Recent finds, offline receipts and rates, Prestige Offering and offline-income progress, earn-coins missions, coin and gem upgrade benefits, the tutorial's "N more coins needed", and written amounts in Prestige descriptions and the Merge All hint (commits `d2fe421`, `5f64ba4`).
2. The wallet could round up: the old `fmt_currency` used `NumberFormat.number`, which rounds half up, so 1,999 coins showed "2.00K". Balances now round down and prices round up (`d2fe421`).
3. The skill cooldown formula existed twice (cast and getter); now there is one getter (`d2fe421`).
4. The rain look was hard-coded in the weather script; it now lives in `WeatherStyle` resources (`d2fe421`, tuned in `7a194e9`).

## Remaining issues and recommendations (not changed here)

- **Provenance:** a licence record for `assets/sounds` (and the PixelPlains purchase) is needed before release.
- **Tuning still in code:** skill reach, the 80% Heal threshold, enemy chase speeds and the leash could move into a combat-rules resource next to `fog_rules.tres`.
- **Historical probes** (audit, balance, evolution_farming, farming_ui, feedback, group_progress, inspector) still fail by design and are kept as history; deleting them would reduce noise.
- **Balance:** the requested skill slowdown lowers player DPS; see the Change 13 Notes. The developer judges on the device.

## Android-only checks

Rain visibility and clutter at zooms 1, 2 and 3 and the overcast tone against the reference; frame time on a low-end phone in a rainy map and in a final-boss fight with helpers; legibility of the gold World banner on bosses and map markers; fit of the Next stage line under the minimap on narrow and tall screens; shortened currency labels in buttons; the summon sound in the mix; how the final boss, the summoners and the slower skills feel.
