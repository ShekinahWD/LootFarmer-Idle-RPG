## Core loop and controls

Joystick movement, then 0.5 seconds idle before target acquisition/approach/attack. Keep current target until dead or player movement; chain nearby eligible targets. Manual target drag uses line/arrow feedback. Camera drag/pinch/zoom slider; joystick returns camera to player. Manual movement cancels auto-farming focus. Focus chooses enemy type and variant and can return after death. Player HP is red; level bar blue/white. Player starts at level 1; existing level ceiling 1000 remains the baseline.

Death costs time, not possessions. Respawn at selected owned camp. Out of combat (five seconds without being hit, no aggressive enemy) the player recovers 1% max HP per second anywhere and 10% per second beside an owned camp of the current stage; nothing recovers in combat, so Heal and potions keep their roles. Purchasing a camp does not silently select it. Combat effects/rewards are simulation-owned once per action; animation/audio never apply a second hit. Panels do not pause combat; explicit global pause freezes simulation. Settings/panel navigation remains usable while paused. Cancel pending windups on load. Hard Reset clears progression, including keys/cards, and retains settings.

Use Kenney Fantasy UI and supplied PixelPlains content. Keep visual composition inspector-editable where robust. Long panels scroll with pinned actions, safe-area layout and proper touch arbitration. Do not restore retired Cartoon GUI styling. Remove permanent overhead variant-description text only; names/HP remain. Tutorial may explain variants. Recent finds remain top-left (last three, expandable to ten; no coin spam).

Totals: 34 normal appearance/type identities and 14 bosses; 48 characters. Variants and spawned instances do not increase those totals. The count/gate/season structure is approved; exact character placement is delegated authoring in Content and Balance. Winter I is the smallest tutorial map; each second seasonal stage is larger than its first, Spring II largest. Fixed encounter levels, no player-level scaling. Weather is cosmetic; winter blue-leaf animation replaces rain. Sparks are available optional polish, not additional gameplay or an approved replacement for summer rain.

Stages 4, 6 and 7 require both designated bosses. Stages 1–3 and 5 require one gate boss. Credit required kills once per run; respawns do not relock exits. Earlier areas remain accessible for farming. Each unlocked stage has a free safe arrival camp; additional camps cost coins. Travel between unlocked camps is free. **No teleport during combat:** block all camp/stage travel while the player has a pending attack, took a hit within five seconds, or a living enemy is aggroed/winding up. Death, explicit pause, app suspension and an unfinished prestige batch also block travel. Respawning after death remains automatic at the selected camp. Stage access/camps reset on prestige.

Spring II completion requires defeating each of its six normal enemy types at least once in that run and all three bosses, not an empty map. One boss is clearly superior and final. First approaching it blocks combat and discovers the final main mission. Only then do the other 13 bosses begin granting guaranteed unique keys, one per boss; earlier kills do not count. All 13 keys unlock final-boss combat. Keys and mission discovery survive prestige; no second approach is needed to reactivate key earning. The final objective rewards once; farming remains afterward.

## Map and discovery

Fog (Change 8): explored cells are permanent and saved; the walking player explores a close 48-unit radius (a third of Change 7's 144). Momentary vision is 2× that radius (96): unexplored terrain, enemies and loot inside it show through a lighter veil and can be fought, but vision is never saved and never discovers an encounter spot. Camps are landmarks: sighted from 400 units (remembered per stage, with a "Camp spotted" notice and a 40-unit footprint revealed so the camp shows through the fog). Owned camps reveal 160 around them at unlock and when upgraded even with the player elsewhere. All radii live in resources/game/fog_rules.tres. Camera movement never explores. Player reveal radius upgrades are per stage; camp radius upgrades per camp, both up to +100%. Teleport reveals destination only. Death and stage revisits retain exploration; prestige clears it.

Map detail purchases per stage: base terrain/player/discovered camps/exits; level 1 generic enemy-location markers with bosses distinguished; level 2 identities and inspection; level 3 farming details respecting purchased stat/drop information. Never reveal undiscovered places through upgrades. Markers remember spawn sites, not live remote positions/HP. Boss requirement counters are public (0/2 etc.); unknown boss locations remain hidden. Minimap/expanded map share one disclosure authority, uniform aspect-preserving transforms and hit filtering. Playbook 117.9, 124.12 and 127.6 apply.

## Enemies, variants and loot

Normal types have outfit-only, weapon-only and both variants. Selected types additionally have a fourth extra-strong variant carrying weapon, outfit and skill. It uses the skill and can drop all three through independent rolls. It shares upgrades/achievements with its type and supports focused farming. It remains a normal enemy, with up to six actors per spot from spawn upgrades. First skill variant uses Heal visibly on itself.

Normal skill chance starts at 5%, upgradeable by +15 percentage points. Early bosses use one introduced skill, later bosses two, final boss three complementary skills with clear cues. Boss skill chance starts at 10%, upgradeable to 25%; one skill roll per kill, then choose one of that boss's used skills. No three independent boss skill rolls. Skill drops are base-tier and grow by merging copies of the same skill (Change 8; no skill evolution). **Gear (Change 7):** weapons are shared families — Sword and Axe, each a 7-tier material ladder (Wood, Stone, Iron, Gold, Diamond, Emerald, Obsidian) with its own owned icon and swing — so a Wood Sword is one identity whichever enemy carries or drops it. Outfits stay tied to their enemy's look. Stages 1–2 carry and drop tier 1, 3–4 tier 2, 5–6 tier 3, 7 tier 4 and 8 (Spring II) tier 5 since Change 9, for weapons and outfits alike; every normal outfit of a tier has the same base, and boss-exclusive outfits are 3× a normal outfit of their tier. **Change 8 drops:** every normal enemy on every stage has a 5% base chance for each item it carries, rolled independently (Loot Chance upgrade +0.5 points per level, 15 levels, 12.5% per item); bosses keep 30%. Each boss carries and drops the weapon one tier above its stage's normal enemies, in its own family (Snowman: Stone Axe; Spring II bosses: Emerald Sword since Change 9); enemy damage stays authored by tools/tune_campaign.py, so the weapon is not counted twice. Drops are rolled per individual enemy death. Currency credits immediately; ground items retain source type. No manual selling or auto-sell remains in final design.

## Inventory, merges, evolution and copy bonuses

Shared inventory holds weapons, outfits and nonconsumable skills; consumables have separate capacity. One slot per distinct owned tier identity; duplicates stack without a designed gameplay quantity cap, including when capacity is full. Maximum purchasable capacities derive from the complete catalog including evolutions. Capacity is checked before acquiring a new identity or evolving into a missing identity. One equipped weapon/outfit. Each distinct owned UNEQUIPPED weapon contributes 30% of its stats once, and each unequipped outfit 15% (Change 9), not once per duplicate. The equipped item counts once at full value. Outfit milestones count only for the worn outfit. Weapons stay at 30%: they are 14 shared identities and the only damage scaling (Change 9 Notes). Stored skills confer no passive stats.

Merge cap ^100 for weapons; ^500 for outfits and skills (Change 8), which grow only from copies of themselves. Outfits (Change 9): core = base × (1 + 0.02·min(m,100) + 0.01·max(0,m−100)), so ^100 3×, ^300 5×, ^500 7×. The worn outfit also gains milestones scaled by its tier t: ^100 Guard 3t Defense, ^200 +10% outfit HP, ^300 Recovery 0.05%·t max HP/s, ^400 Guard 6t, ^500 Guard 10t and Recovery 0.08%·t. Skills keep the Change 8 curve (effective levels = 100 + 100·ln(1 + (m−100)/100)). Coefficients live in catalog.progression.merge and outfit_milestones. Same-id duplicates power merges below cap. Merge and random-roll records survive zero stock during the run. At ^100, current spare quantity gives a small bonus; copies are NOT consumed merely to gain this bonus and no reservation bucket exists. Spending/using copies reduces their corresponding bonus. Bonus is quantity-dependent, not a permanent incremental reward. Random rolls do not grow with merges or stock bonuses.

Evolution (weapons only since Change 11; supplies grow by mastery, see Change 11) needs ^100 and at least 101 source copies: consumes 100, retains one source copy and its progression, and grants a successor. New successors start ^0; existing successor progression persists. Successor intrinsic ^0 base stats equal 85% of source ^100 base stats, excluding stock bonuses, random rolls and external player/card buffs. Later merges surpass the predecessor. Same rule for weapons and consumables; tier 7 remains the current authored ceiling. Outfit and skill tiers that no enemy drops remain only so older saves keep evolved copies (catalog `legacy`). Same-art successors use distinct stable ids and tier colors. Source stock bonuses decrease when evolution spends copies. Final-tier stock can still grow. Items and all their progression reset on prestige.

## Skills

Stage 1 no skills; late stage 2 Heal; stage 3 Frost Burst, Fire Burst and Lightning Strike; stage 4 Arcane Focus and Arcane Strike. Stages 5–8 combine these families. Skills use shared inventory with a filter, one equipped slot initially up to three (extra slots 300 / 600 gems since Change 13), no duplicate family across slots. Casts never consume skills. No mana. Cooldown is per family and shared between manual/automatic use; swapping/evolving/re-equipping cannot reset it. Per-slot auto-use unlock/toggle survives prestige. Cooldown upgrade is per family, coins, max 30% reduction and minimum cooldown, resets on prestige.

Attacks require a valid target; Frost Burst affects nearby enemies, Fire Burst target area. Heal auto below 80% HP; Arcane Focus only during combat. Base cooldowns (Change 13): Lightning 8 s, Fire 10 s, Frost 10 s, Strike 10 s, Focus 15 s, Heal 18 s, for player and enemy casts alike. Buff recasts refresh rather than stack unlimited copies. Strength improves by merge/evolution; cooldown improves only through its defined upgrade. Exact skill numbers and VFX/SFX mappings appear in Content and Balance and Asset Registry.

## Missions

Regular daily missions start with 3 slots, expand to 8 (supersedes old 20). Capacity and auto-claim cost gems and persist. New slots fill immediately. No mission-reward multiplier purchase. Regular missions target 15–60 minutes of relevant play, no expiry; local-midnight refresh replaces claimed missions, never unfinished progress or unclaimed rewards. Missed days do not stack. A reward claimed after crossing its eligible refresh fills the slot immediately; claims before refresh wait until refresh. Goals/rewards fixed at assignment; no identical active tasks; only achievable content. Mission rewards cannot recursively satisfy earn-currency goals. The Missions button badge shows only while a Claim button is waiting (Change 14). Cards read In progress (muted chip), Ready to claim (primary Claim button) or Claimed (dimmed, green check, no button, next refresh noted); claimed main objectives stay listed under a Completed objectives toggle.

Main objectives teach progression; completed AND rewarded ones stay completed after prestige. Unfinished main progress resets, except final mission discovery/keys. All unclaimed mission rewards clear on prestige, with no extra claim-first step. Regular missions regenerate for restarted progress. Main objective states are detailed in State Contracts. Mission Specialist is an automatic sequential prestige chain alongside ordinary missions; it never pauses/replaces the board or requires activation.

## Prestige

Eleven paths, three unique cards each; three discoverable one-use opportunities per path. **Prestige entry (Change 8, replaces the Change 6 Winter II gate):** the Prestige system opens after the first boss, the Winter I Snowman, is defeated (a lifetime kill count, so it never relocks after a reset); regular kills do not open it. **Change 10:** the Prestige button is plain navigation in every state and always opens the panel, so opportunities can be browsed and followed from a fresh profile; only the reset action inside the panel is gated. A reset needs the Snowman defeat and at least one ready opportunity that can still award a card; the card is chosen during the reset, never required beforehand. The panel lists both conditions with ✓/✗ and the locked reset button names the first missing one. Opportunities are not repeatable prestige routes. Discovery persists, shows requirements/cards, and emits a discovery notice. All track automatically; Follow merely pins the next step, and only an opportunity in progress can be followed (ready or redeemed ones show no Follow; the action layer refuses it and a follow ends when its opportunity becomes ready). A followed opportunity shows a small lower-left HUD tracker (icon, name, step and next objective with numbers; Ready state once ready) until unfollowed or consumed. Ordinary progress resets on prestige; offline path progress survives. Sequence breaks affect only unfinished sequences; ready opportunities remain secured while farming.

One reset may redeem multiple ready opportunities. Choose one remaining card per opportunity, sequentially; same-path options shrink 3 → 2 → 1, no duplicates. Consume all redeemed opportunities and reset once after choices. Completed paths stop offering opportunities; keep history. Eleven same-path and six mixed three-distinct-card combos each grant a small permanent bonus once. 33 card awards do not imply 33 resets. First opportunity target 1–2 hours. Collecting all cards and world completion are separate goals. Cards are presented as visual cards (owned Kenney frame tinted per path, PixelPlains path art, large computed value, one line of meaning); a card is selected by tapping and kept through a confirmation that states what is kept and reset. Discovery, completion, each card and the reset get a short queued celebration banner (owned star and spark frames) and a persistent badge on the Prestige button. Since Change 12 the sound plays at the milestone itself, not when a held banner is shown (see Change 12 audio). See authored catalog and durable transaction contract.

## Offline

Fixed authored rates, unrelated to recent combat performance. Duration starts 5 hours, max 48. Separate permanent coin/gem rate purchases and weapon/outfit/consumable unlocks/rate upgrades. Only identities with positive inventory stock at departure are eligible for offline item grants; no offline skills or new discoveries. Stack duplicates; selling no longer applies. Offline grants never advance ordinary missions or non-offline prestige progress. Dedicated offline path: cumulative 72 hours, continuous 24 hours, offline-income milestone. Time beyond earnings cap counts; all three progress records persist across prestige. **Claim (Change 7):** returning from the background or launching records the earnings as a saved pending claim (several absences join; each interval keeps its own cap); nothing is paid and no offline opportunity is discovered until the player presses Claim on the Welcome back panel in game, and the claim clears the pending record in the same durable write. ## Change 5 opening and presentation contract

Winter I enemies only retaliate against the player who attacks them; no proximity activation or assistance. This includes both first enemy types. Winter II introduces individual proximity aggression. Summer I introduces assistance limited to living allies of the same type and variant within 110 world units. The first enemy restores prototype baseline 18 HP, 2 damage and a 2-second attack interval; the remainder retains campaign tuning pending playtests.

Heal is reusable sustain: tier-1 10 HP (Change 6; was 6), 18-second cooldown, at most 12% maximum HP per cast. Potion is burst recovery: tier-1 25 HP, consumes one copy, 5-second potion cooldown (Change 6; was 2), usable during Heal cooldown. Both clamp to missing HP. Heal is available below 80% HP; potion at or below 80%. Enemy Heal restores at most 8% maximum HP. Most bosses render at twice normal scale, final boss 2.5×; bee and squirrel keep normal scale. Existing scenery marks all boss arenas. Skill effects render below actors.

First profile chooses Guided, Overview or None. Change 6 guided lessons follow the real route across surfaces with an owned arrow pointer and frame (HUD button, then the panel tab, then the exact control), point only at visible controls and known places, and ask for an attainable action when coins or discovery are missing (explore, earn N coins). Guidance never takes input except an introduction card's Got it button; an unrelated panel, confirmation, pause, prestige choice or celebration hides it entirely, and it returns when that surface closes. Guided lessons follow movement, combat, collection, equipment, merge, upgrade and camp state; unrelated panels/confirmations suspend guidance. Later proximity, skill and group behavior have contextual cards. Guidance preferences persist through prestige; Hard Reset restores the chooser. Tutorial choices do not alter combat rules. See the audit for remaining first-profile progression and device acceptance gaps.

## Change 6 contract summary

- **Encounter layout:** every stage places its weakest normal type nearest the arrival camp, difficulty rising with distance, and every boss farther than every normal type, in its existing landmark arena (moved with its court). Variant spots are 100 units from the type center on both axes; no actor of one spot can be auto-acquired (110 + reach) from another spot, and camps sit outside every spot's acquisition range. tools/layout_campaign_groups.py owns and checks these rules.
- **First boss:** the Snowman is a later encounter in the far arena; it needs roughly level 10–14 with merged gear (measured 15–29 min ordinary/rush, 10–19 expert). Its multiplier is an explicit override; other bosses use the default boss multipliers in Content and Balance.
- **Recovery roles:** Heal tier 1 restores 10 HP (cap 12% max HP) every 18 s; potions restore 25 HP with a 5 s cooldown, so bought supply cannot be chain-drunk through a boss. Out-of-combat recovery and camp resting are above.
- **Panels:** one bottom-anchored sheet over the map, sized to its content, capped to the map and scrolling only when needed, with primary actions pinned in a fixed bar. Item details compare against the equipped item with effective deltas and browse with owned previous/next arrows in inventory order (ends disable the arrow; no wrap). Feedback while a panel is open uses its fixed notice line; otherwise a queued toast on a dark backing.
- **Navigation (Change 8):** World (camps, map, travel, map upgrades), Missions, Prestige, Upgrades (Player Upgrades; also on tapping the character), Settings. Stats moved into Settings. Badges: green ! for claimable missions or ready prestige, gold ✦ for an unseen discovery.

## Change 14 additions

- **Map information cluster (top right, top to bottom):** the zoom slider, "Map Explored: n%", the minimap, "Next Stage · Bosses n/m" ("World clear · n/9" on Spring II). Both lines sit on a compact dark plate (the HudChip style: the Kenney backing panel at 86% dark) at 18 / 19 px, share the minimap's right edge and never take touches. - **Enemy research:** the Upgrades tab of a discovered enemy lists Reveal Stats and Reveal Drops under Research, the same cards and purchase as Overview (Max once owned). Overview keeps them until bought, then shows the information.
- **Joystick:** same place, size and behaviour; a restrained dark backplate inside the ring, a more opaque ring and a dark rim around the knob make it readable on snow and on dark rainy maps. Colours are scene-node modulates.
- **Focused Farming guidance:** the profile's first Focused Farming purchase says "Focused Farming unlocked" (every onboarding mode). Except under "None", the guide then points at the next real tap: the Farm button in that enemy's panel (with one line under it explaining it), else the HUD Upgrades button for that enemy, else the minimap. It ends when Farm is turned on, on Got it, after 45 s on screen, or on another map. Never shown twice (a "focus_unlocked" seen flag, kept by Prestige, cleared by Hard Reset); the hint itself is never saved.

## Change 13 additions

- **Auto-Merge by exact item:** a type's Auto-Merge covers exactly what it drops (its loot weapon and outfit, the base skill items of its skills). Tiers evolved from those items follow their own droppers: two Stone Swords evolved from Wood Swords stay unmerged until a type that itself drops the Stone Sword has Auto-Merge, and then every owned Stone Sword merges. Shared drops merge through any of their droppers; supplies never.
- **Auto Evolution:** a one-time unlock per enemy type for 2,000 gems (the same on every map), beside Auto-Merge in the type's Upgrades. Once a second it evolves, by the manual rule (^100, 101 copies, a free slot for a new successor), the weapons that type drops, one tier at a time. Never outfits, skills, supplies or the last tier; it grants no Auto-Merge to the new tier and does not manage tiers the type does not drop. Kept by Prestige, removed by Hard Reset.
- **Health Potion:** 12 s base cooldown, mastery shortens it to 10.8 / 10.2 / 10 s, never below 10 s. Heal amount unchanged. Heal stays the free sustain between potions.
- **Skills:** automatic casting 100 / 250 / 500 gems; second and third skill slots 300 / 600 gems. Cooldowns as listed under Skills. Item details show each skill's effective cooldown.
- **Rainy maps (Summer II, Spring II):** denser rain (still world-anchored) and an overcast tint on the map art measured from the supplied reference; actors, HP bars, loot, effects, fog and camps are not tinted. Values in resources/game/weather_*.tres. Other maps unchanged.
- **Next-stage guidance:** under the minimap, "Next stage · Bosses n/m" (Change 14: "Next Stage", on a dark chip) (or "World clear · n/9" on Spring II) from the run's gate kills; hidden when nothing is pending. World lists each gate boss as defeated or still needed ("Undiscovered boss" until found). A discovered boss that still counts carries a gold World banner above its HP bar and on its map and minimap marker; undiscovered bosses never do.
- **Currency:** every Coin and Gem amount uses one format: exact below 1,000 (up to two decimals below 100), then 1K, 1.5K, 12.34K, 1M… Balances and rewards round down, prices round up; purchases use exact values. Camp prices no longer show raw numbers.
- **Summoning:** the Grim Reaper (Horned Hood), the Sunflower (Sprout) and the final Green Alien (Green Cap) call helpers after a short telegraph: 2 per call, at most 2 / 2 / 3 alive, every 22 / 24 / 20 s. Helpers are weaker, violet-tinted copies of a normal type of the same map, give no rewards or progress of any kind, are never saved, and dissolve when their summoner dies or leaves the fight, the player dies, or the map changes. Values in resources/game/enemy_abilities.tres.
- **Final boss:** an action every 11 s. Below 50% HP it heals first (4% of max HP) whenever Heal is ready; otherwise a random ready skill from Fire, Strike, Frost, Lightning, or Focus. It also summons. HP, damage, drops, keys and completion unchanged. No Rage bar (no rule it would show). Balance is first-pass; see the Change 13 Notes.

## Change 12 additions

- **Cyan Robot Heal:** an enemy or boss casts Heal only below 80% of its maximum HP. A boss checks this before its 0.6 s telegraph, so at 80%+ HP no Heal effect plays (it used to telegraph the effect every 1.6 s and heal nothing), and the turn passes to its next skill instead of blocking the rotation. A successful Heal restores 8% of the caster's maximum HP (player Heal: min(power, 12% max HP)), clamped to missing HP, starts the 18 s cooldown, plays one Heal effect on the caster's feet (a child of its view, so it follows movement) and one green "+n" number, centred above the head and 1.3× the size of damage numbers. A caster killed during the telegraph never heals.
- **Map explored:** the gameplay HUD shows "Map explored: n%" (Change 14: "Map Explored: n%" on a dark chip between the zoom slider and the minimap; no longer in the lower-right corner; it never takes touches). n = permanently explored cells ÷ the map's fog grid, rounded down, so 100% means every cell. Momentary sight and camera panning never count. The same value (`campaign.explored_percent`) feeds the Regional opportunities and their 90% rule.
- **Settings:** the credits line no longer mentions the font or calls the game premium. Sound & music: Music volume and Sound effects volume sliders (0–100%, 0 = silent), Mute gameplay sounds (taps, purchases, merges, combat casts) and Mute special sounds (milestones and notifications), each independent of the other; the sound-effect volume applies to both categories. Saved with the other settings; Prestige and Hard Reset keep settings.
- **Audio:** one cue set (resources/game/audio_cues.tres, inspector-editable) lists the music track and every semantic cue with its category, priority and cooldown. Callers request a cue id; the audio service plays only the highest-priority audible cue requested in a frame, so one transition never stacks sounds, and the music holds its place while paused. Gameplay: click, confirm (a successful manual purchase, claim, merge, evolution or card), the six skill casts. Special: Prestige discovered, followed-step completed, mission ready, Prestige ready, new map, Prestige. Repetitive simulation (hits, kills, pickups, automation) is silent.
- **Special sounds:** a newly discovered Prestige opportunity; each completed step of the followed opportunity (Expedition/Specialist steps, Collection outfits, Trophy bosses; once per step at the transition, never on load or for steps already done; the final step plays the Prestige-ready cue); a mission reaching its goal when it will be claimed by hand (silent while auto-claim is bought and on); a new map; the Prestige itself.
- **Merge All:** a one-time 5,000-Gem unlock in Upgrades → Inventory, kept by Prestige, removed by Hard Reset. Filters then shows "Merge All · n spare copies"; one tap performs, for every weapon, outfit and skill, the same merge as the item's own Merge spare copies (one copy kept, never past ^100 / ^500), never evolves and never touches supplies. One aggregate notice ("Merged n copies across m items") and one confirm sound; "Nothing to merge" disables it. Using it is free. Timed gear auto-merge is unrelated and still bought per type.
- **Stone-tier swing:** the Winter I Snowman carries and drops the **Stone Axe** (tier 2, gray stone icon). The animation pack has no Stone set, so the tier used the dark Onix swing, which read as a black axe. Stone Axe and Stone Sword now swing with the Iron set sheet in a warm-gray tint (catalog `attack_tint`, applied to the swing sprite only); Iron keeps the plain Iron set. Timing, range and damage are unchanged.
- **Camera after Prestige:** performing a Prestige (not opening its panel) recentres the camera on the newly spawned player, restores follow and clears any drag; zoom is kept.
- **Gem chance:** base 5% (normal and boss), Gem Chance 20 levels of +0.5 points (prices 1–12 unchanged; 13–20: 560, 660, 770, 890, 1,020, 1,160, 1,310, 1,470), Gem Wisdom +5 points, kill achievements up to +4 points per type, Boss Gem Chance +5 × 8 on bosses. The 20% / 60% caps are removed; only 0–100% clamping. Gem amounts unchanged.

Coin upgrades keep their own Keep/Reset rule. Each upgrade has one currency, so nothing is split. Hard Reset clears all upgrades. Unspent Gems still reset as before. Gem Chance, Gem Amount, Boss Gem Chance, Boss Gem Amount, Spawn Amount and Spawn Cooldown used to reset despite the "gem upgrades are kept" text; kept spawn levels now apply to the fresh world at once.

- **Ground loot** expires after 300 s of active time again (fade in the last 30 s, frozen while paused, in the background or on another map).
- **Supplies:** five supplies, one inventory identity each (no tiers, no evolution), and supply capacity equals that count: 1 slot + 4 purchases = 5.
