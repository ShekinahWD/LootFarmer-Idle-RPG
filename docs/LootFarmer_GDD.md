# LootFarmer — Game Design Document — current (2026-11-16, Change 85)

## Change 85 — Enemy skill icons show every usable skill (16 November 2026)

Display only; save schema, version, gameplay and balance unchanged. Probe: `tests/change_85_probe` (18 checks; a windowed run saves `generated/evidence/change_85/green_alien_icons_540x960.png`, ignored by Git).

- **Rule.** The icons over an enemy are every skill it can actually use (`campaign.shown_skills`, read by `world._skill_icons`). An enemy casts from one of two sources: the Change 78 rotation (its definition's skill list, `sim.enemy_definition`, so variants without skills and summoned helpers show nothing) or, for the final boss only, the Change 13 kit (Heal, then `campaign.final_skill_pool`: the attack pool Fire, Strike, Frost, Lightning, then Focus). `final_choice` now reads the same `final_skill_pool` (same order and filter as before), so the icons and the AI cannot drift apart.
- **Green Alien** shows six: Focus, Fire, Strike, Heal, Frost, Lightning (its catalog list first, then the rest of its kit). Change 84 had added only Heal.
- **Audit.** Every other type and variant (191 pairs) already showed exactly its rotation, and each shown skill is selectable by it; the Green Alien was the only type with usable skills missing. Summoning (Grim Reaper, Sunflower, Green Alien) is a separate ability with no skill icon, as before.
- **Unchanged gameplay.** Skill drops, mastery and the map marker keep the catalog list; damage, cooldowns (10 s boss standard) and activation conditions are untouched.
- **Layout.** The icon row holds as many icons as fit the HP bar's width (at least 2, so every one- and two-icon row is unchanged) and wraps upward, centred, into further rows when more are needed. The Green Alien's six fit its 80-unit bar in one 73-unit row (about 262 px on a 540-wide portrait screen at the default zoom), stacked between its name and the target marker / World banner, clear of the HUD rails.
- **Supersedes:** Change 84's four-icon Green Alien row.

## Change 84 — Green Alien Heal icon and attack 900 → 700 (16 November 2026)

Two corrections; save schema, game version and Android version code unchanged. Catalog writer: `tools/change84_catalog.py`. Probe: `tests/change_84_probe` (25 checks).

- **Heal icon.** The icons over an enemy are its catalog `skills` list, but the Green Alien's Heal never came from that list: its Change 13 final kit (`final_choice`: Heal first below 50% while off cooldown, then the attack pool and Focus) and Rage's immediate Heal cast it regardless. Its list stayed `focus, fire, strike`, so Heal worked but had no icon. `campaign.shown_skills` now gives the head row the catalog list plus Heal for the final boss (`world._skill_icons`); every other type's row is unchanged. Display only: its skill drops, mastery and map icon keep the catalog list (no new Heal drop). Healing is unchanged: 4% of max HP below 50%, telegraphed, the 10 s boss cooldown; Rage +20% and its one immediate Heal; Boss Breaker still reduces it.
- **Attack ×7/9.** A hit on the player is definition damage × guard / boss guard / Focus × 100 / (100 + player defense), all multiplicative, and Rage changes only the boss's attack speed, damage taken and healing, never its hit. So the reported ~900 becomes ~700 by scaling the definition damage by 7/9 through `boss_tuning` `[0.6, 3.252]` → `[0.6, 2.5293]`: attack **1,245.5378 → 968.7388** per hit (900 → 700.0). Its damage skills stay definition damage × skill power, so they follow the hit (×7/9), as they followed the Change 83 doubling. HP (182,708.2142), interval (1.3 s; 0.867 s in Rage), defense, Heal, summons and every other type are unchanged.
- **Supersedes:** Change 83's Green Alien attack 1,245.5378 (HP unchanged). `tools/change79/81/83_catalog.py` know the new value.

## Change 83 — Green Alien finale: Rage, warning, soundtrack, celebration, ending, Game Completion (16 November 2026)

Save schema 9 unchanged. New saved fields, none implied by older state: `final_warning_ok`, `final_experience`, `final_celebrated`, `final_ending_seen` (booleans) and `completion_maxed` (list); all are kept by Prestige and cleared by Hard Reset, and an older save reads them as false / empty. The game version and Android version code are not touched. Brief: `docs/LootFarmer_Change_83.md` plus two written clarifications (temporary recommended volume; music after victory). Catalog writer: `tools/change83_catalog.py`. Probe: `tests/change_83_probe` (108 checks). Capture: `tests/change_83_capture` → `generated/evidence/change_83/<shape>/` (stills, 10 fps sequence, playback MP4; ignored by Git).

- **Green Alien ×2 (the only balance change).** Through its `boss_tuning` entry `[0.3, 1.626]` → `[0.6, 3.252]`: HP **91,354.1071 → 182,708.2142**, attack **622.7689 → 1,245.5378** per hit. Interval (1.3 s), respawn, skills, summons, rewards and every other type are unchanged. Its damage skills are definition damage × skill power, so they double once with the hit. **Heal** already existed (Change 13, unchanged): below 50% HP it heals 4% of max HP (now 7,308) with the 10 s boss cooldown, telegraphed; Boss Breaker still reduces it.
- **Rage Mode.** The first time the living, fighting Green Alien is at or below **20%** HP after taking damage (checked in the step, so a lethal blow never enrages it): attacks start **1.5× as often** (interval 1.3 → 0.867 s), it takes damage **÷ 1.3** (every enemy's Defense is 0, so "+30% defense" is the game's own mitigation multiplier, the one Guard uses for the player; it applies to basic attacks, skills and Cleave), and each Heal restores **+20%** (4.8%). On entry it heals once at once (4.8%, no telegraph) and that Heal starts the normal 10 s Heal cooldown. The latch `actor.rage` is saved with the actor: it survives save/load, the player's death, its own regeneration and the arena reopening, and is dropped only when that boss dies; the next Green Alien starts normal. Entry: world moment "rage" (red flash, crimson shockwave, red god-rays, rings, embers), a camera shake, cue `final_enraged`, and a red warning banner for 5 s ("GREEN ALIEN IS ENRAGED! / Attack speed +50% · Defense +30% · Healing +20%", never modal). A ground aura (`rage_fx.gd`: two soft red glows, a crimson ground ring, 14 embers) stays under the boss while it is enraged.
- **Red arena barrier.** While the arena's boss is enraged the whole wall (ring, halos, dashes, hexagon cells, light shafts, contact flare) blends to crimson over 0.6 s, with a thin normal-blend crimson core and dark edge under it so it reads on bright grass. Radius, collision and blocking are unchanged; a new closing takes the colour of the boss it holds.
- **Danger warning (once per profile, also for older saves).** The first valid attempt to engage (walking or Auto Farm within 88 with 13 keys, or a tap there) shows a blocking panel before any key visual, arena, combat or music: "FINAL BOSS — DANGER" — no escape once the arena seals. Buttons: **Set Recommended Volume** (temporary mix, does not answer), **Yes, Fight!**, **No, Not Yet**. The world is held while it is open; Back is No. No closes it with nothing changed (a Focus on the Green Alien stops); it asks again after the player has walked 60 beyond the radius, or at once on a tap, never every frame. Yes saves `final_warning_ok` and plays the **full Change 82 ceremony**. Only when the ceremony completes is `final_experience` saved (with `final_unsealed`); an interrupted ceremony replays on the next approach without asking. Profiles that unsealed or defeated the Green Alien before this build see the warning and the ceremony once; keys, kills and rewards are never touched. Later fights skip both. `final_locked` now also covers a profile without `final_experience`.
- **Soundtrack (`final_audio.gd`, derived from the state every frame).** `EndGame - Music 1 (1).ogg` plays while the unlocked Green Alien is really engaged (never during the warning, ceremony or a cancelled approach), crossfading over 1.2 s to `EndGame - Music 1 (2).ogg` when it enrages. Victory, the player's death, a reset or a map change release it at once (0.5 s fade) and the main track returns from where it held; the celebration always plays over the main music. Encounter tracks loop, follow the Music volume and pause, and use one pair of crossfade players in `AudioService` (`set_boss_music`). Laughter: one of `Laugh 01/03/05.wav` at random, first after 8–14 s, then every 18–32 s of unpaused fighting, never within 6 s of the enrage cue, never while held, sealed, celebrating or out of the fight (a local RNG; the simulation's RNG is not consumed). Mix: battle tracks −13 / −12.5 dB against the main track's −8 dB (measured means −8.5 / −9.3 vs −16.9 dB), enrage +3 dB, laughs 0 dB.
- **Recommended audio (opt-in, temporary).** Only an explicit tap (the danger panel's button, or the small **"Recommended Audio / Tap to Apply"** chip under Boss Keys in the right rail, shown at the living Green Alien within 300 or during its fight) sets **Music 40% / Sound Effects 100%**; tapping again undoes it. The player's own values are remembered and never saved as the mix (the save writes the originals), so a closed app cannot keep it. It stays for the encounter and through the first victory's 10 s celebration, and is restored when the fight ends otherwise: a later victory, the player's death, No on the warning, or the player no longer at the boss. A channel the player changed in the meantime is kept. Mutes are never touched.
- **Boss Keys quest (Missions → Main Objectives, every map).** From the discovery (never before) until the victory: a card "Final Boss · Green Alien" with "Boss keys: X / 13", a bar, the explanation and one row per boss grouped by map in catalog order: "✓ <boss> · key collected" or "Defeat <boss> — <map>". At 13/13 it reads "All keys collected! Return to Green Alien — Spring II"; after the victory, "Green Alien defeated · main adventure complete". The Boss Keys HUD chip opens it. Key acquisition is unchanged. No new map pins (the chip is the persistent tracker; the follow pill keeps the player's own followed objective).
- **Final-boss marker.** In `map_view.gd` (minimap and World map): 1.4× a boss marker (1.75× a normal one), a soft pulsing green glow, a double green/gold border and a gold crown; tap reach grows with it; the spacing fit counts its size. It shows only where the Green Alien's spot is discovered and it is alive. Its World type chip shows its portrait in the same green. Other markers are unchanged.
- **First victory.** The arena opens and rewards resolve as before; then a **10 s celebration** with the world frozen (like the ceremony; pause and background pause it; travel, Teleport and Prestige refused with "Enjoy the celebration first!"): a gold banner "THE GREEN ALIEN IS DEFEATED!", the camera frames the floor above the joystick and eases back to the player's own zoom. The boss respawn clock waits while the celebration runs or the ending is owed. At 10 s `final_celebrated` is saved and the **ending panel** opens (world held): "CONGRATULATIONS! You defeated the Green Alien and completed LootFarmer's main adventure…" with **Continue Playing**, **Leave an Honest Review** (the build's storefront through the review flow; Google Play `com.shekinahwelefuldesigns.lootfarmeridlerpg` on Android) and **Join Our Discord** (`https://discord.gg/JKjeP6PDAU`, now the single configured invite, also in Settings). Any of them saves `final_ending_seen` once; Back is Continue. A reload during the celebration saves nothing (the next victory celebrates again); a celebrated but unacknowledged ending is shown 3 s into the next victory, without the cinematic. Once acknowledged it never returns.
- **Celebration floor (`dance_floor.gd`).** On the Green Alien's map from its defeat until it respawns (state-derived: reload or return shows it, respawn, map change or Prestige removes it): a round disco floor at its home, rim lights, four turning light beams, falling confetti and fireworks (every ~0.7 s during the first celebration, ~2.4 s after). Every obtainable outfit the profile owns (in the inventory or worn; one per outfit) appears one by one within 5 s and dances (hop, sway, turn, walk-row shuffle) in rings around the floor; new outfits join. Below it: "Outfits dancing: X / Y" (Y = 48, every outfit an enemy drops) and "Collect every outfit to complete this celebration floor!", wrapped to the floor width. Later victories: the floor only, nothing modal, no camera, play continues. One Sprite2D per dancer, 40 confetti, three 24-particle firework emitters.
- **Game Completion (`completion.gd`).** 100% needs all four parts, **25% each**: Map Mastery objectives (40: 8 maps × 5, progress = Mastery's own partial value), Prestige cards owned (48), Player and map upgrades at their cap (53 tracks: every global upgrade with ranks except the retired `skill_auto_0/1/2` and the rankless `coin_chance`, plus Map Details and Reveal Radius on all 8 maps), enemy upgrades at their cap (726 tracks: per type, the Enemy panel's Rewards, Automation and Research lists with its availability rules). Inside a part each track counts equally (earned ranks / cap). A track that reached its cap is latched in `completion_maxed`, so a Prestige reset of Coin upgrades does not take completion back; an older save counts from its current ranks. Exactly 100 only when every part is complete, otherwise at most 99; announced once ("100% GAME COMPLETION!"). Shown in Map Mastery (overall bar and each part's done / total / %), Stats ("Game completion" now this value, plus "Map Mastery"), and World ("Map Mastery n% · Game Completion n%"). Map Mastery's own total, its "all maps" latch and the Master Farmer reward are unchanged (the banner now says "Every map mastered"). Main adventure (Green Alien) and outfits are tracked separately. The Road Ahead tutorial card names the four parts.
- **Assets.** `assets/sounds/EndGame - Music 1 (1).ogg`, `(2).ogg` (imported with loop on), `Enraged - Long 01.wav`, `Laugh 01/03/05.wav`, supplied by the developer for this change; no licence record is retained with them (REL-02). Firework pops reuse the shipped `arcane_blast_01.ogg`. All in the export allowlist (349 files), with the new scripts.
- **Localization:** 42 new English keys pending as `change-83` (`tools/change83_strings.json`); retired `MASTERY_OPEN`, `MASTERY_GAME`, `MASTERY_INTRO`, `MILESTONE_MASTERY_GAME`, `GUIDE_TUTORIAL_FINAL_DETAIL` (replaced by new keys with the four-part meaning).
- **Supersedes:** Change 81/82 Green Alien HP and damage (×2); Change 82 "after its one-time ceremony" now means after the Change 83 warning and ceremony; Change 15 "Game completion" (Map Mastery alone). Test fixtures that fight the Green Alien also set `final_experience` and `final_warning_ok`.

## Change 82 — Green Alien unsealing ceremony and final boss arena (16 November 2026)

Presentation and encounter rules only: no HP, damage, attack rate, skill, summon, reward, key, XP, equipment or Prestige value changed. Save schema 9 unchanged; one new saved flag, `final_unsealed` (no migration; a save without it reads false). The game version and Android version code are not touched. Brief: `docs/LootFarmer_Change_82_Green_Alien_Ceremony_and_Arena.md`. Probe: `tests/change_82_probe` (83 checks). Capture: `tests/change_82_capture` → `generated/evidence/change_82/<shape>/` (stills, a 10 fps frame sequence, playback MP4s; ignored by Git).

- **Two separate states.** The ceremony is once per profile (`state.final_unsealed`, saved, kept by Prestige, cleared by Hard Reset). The arena is per attempt (`campaign.arena`, transient, never saved, rebuilt from the saved actors). The 13-key rule is unchanged and `state.keys` is never consumed or re-awarded.
- **Locked until the ceremony.** With 13 keys and no `final_unsealed`, the Green Alien stays non-hostile and untargetable (`campaign.final_locked`; Auto Farm may still walk to it). Fewer than 13 keys: the Change 81 seal, explanation and tracker are unchanged. Reaching 13 keys on another map plays nothing.
- **Trigger.** The first time the living Green Alien is in sight and within **88** of the player (walking, Auto Farm or a tap within that distance). A tap from farther away shows "All 13 Boss Keys collected! The Green Alien is ready to be challenged." An older save that already has 13 keys sees the ceremony on its first approach.
- **Ceremony (5.6 s, simulation clock).** The whole world is frozen while it plays: no AI, damage, regeneration, clocks or input. Travel, Teleport and Prestige are refused. Pause and background pause it.
  - 0–0.65 s: 13 gold keys (the Change 81 `Key - Gold` art) pop in one by one on a gold ground ring.
  - 0.4–2.9 s: they orbit the hero on an ellipse at body height (keys on the far side are drawn behind the hero), speeding up and brightening, with star sparkles.
  - 2.9–3.45 s: they gather into a knot above the head.
  - 3.45–4.1 s: one after another, they streak along arcs into the boss.
  - 4.1 s: the seal breaks (world moment "unseal": white flash, green shockwave, green and gold god-rays, rings, sparks), a camera shake, and the gold banner **"YOU HAVE PROVEN YOUR WORTH!" / "The Green Alien accepts your challenge. Defeat it!"** with the boss art.
  - 5.6 s: `final_unsealed` is set and saved, then the boss engages (aggro) and the arena closes.
  - During the ceremony the camera frames the hero and the boss above the joystick, then eases back.
  - A reload, Hard Reset or Prestige before 5.6 s drops the ceremony without saving anything, and the next approach replays it. After completion it never plays again (repeat attempts, deaths, reloads, Prestige).
  - Audio: `final_keys` (charge) and `final_unseal` (shield break), gameplay category, existing streams.
- **Arena geometry.** A circle of radius **118** around the Green Alien's home (1032, 253 on Spring II). This is the natural clearing there: the nearest solid is 142 away, so nothing solid is inside and the barrier never crosses a tree or the map edge. 236 across holds melee, Strike (80), Fire and Focus (120) and the helpers' summon ring (40). If the player engages from just outside it (a ranged hit), the circle slides toward them by at most 100 so they are inside. From farther away it waits. The boss always stays within 208 of home, under its 220 leash, so a fight inside never resets by leash.
- **Closing.** It closes when the unsealed boss is alive and engaged and the living player is at least 12 inside the line. Barrier: a green-gold energy ring with halo, turning dashes, hexagon cells (`Hexagon_001`) and short light shafts. It is additive and drawn above the fog. It shrinks in from wider with a flash. The camera pulls back for 1.6 s to show the whole circle, then returns to the player's own zoom and follow (the zoom setting is untouched). Cue `arena_close` (shield up). A re-engagement after a death shows "The arena is sealed! Defeat the Green Alien or fall trying."
- **Escape rules (all in the simulation).** The player, the Green Alien and its helpers are held 10 inside the line in `sim._move`, the one movement path for the joystick, chasing, Auto Farm walking and the post-respawn return. A corrected point inside solid scenery keeps the actor where it was. Every other enemy is held 10 outside and cannot be targeted across the line. Camp Teleport, map travel and Prestige are refused (`ARENA_SEALED`, "The arena is sealed. Win the fight or fall to leave."). The notice shows at most once per 4 s while the player presses the stick against the barrier, and a white flare marks the contact point. Auto Farm cannot be pointed at another type. A Focus set earlier never pulls the hero out, and attackers inside are always answered. Pause, Settings, supplies, skills and choosing a respawn camp stay available. LootFarmer has no simulated knockback (the hit recoil is visual only).
- **Opening.** The arena opens in the same step as a player death (every enemy drops aggro; normal respawn follows) or the boss's defeat (the normal boss-defeated, key and quest flow continues). It also opens on a boss reset or a replaced profile (reload, Prestige, Hard Reset). It swells outward and fades over 0.7 s with cue `arena_open` (portal), and its nodes are hidden. The next engagement after a death closes it again, without the ceremony.
- **Save / reload.** Nothing about the barrier is saved. A reload drops it at once and rebuilds it on the next step only if the saved boss is still engaged and the player is inside. A reload never marks the Green Alien defeated or spends keys.
- **Localization:** 4 new English keys pending as `change-82` (`tools/change82_strings.json`): `FINAL_UNSEAL_TITLE`, `FINAL_UNSEAL_BODY`, `ARENA_CLOSED`, `ARENA_SEALED`.
- **Supersedes:** Change 81's "with all 13 keys the Green Alien can be challenged" now means after its one-time ceremony. Test fixtures that set 13 keys to fight it also set `final_unsealed` (campaign_probe, change_9_balance, change_13 probe/capture, change_36 audit, change_81_probe).

## Change 81 — Final boss clarity, boss keys, Spring combat, endgame progression (16 November 2026)

Save schema 9 unchanged (two new one-time migrations, `camp_radius_global` and `final_intro_seen`, plus a level clamp on load). The game version and Android version code are not touched by this change. Brief: `docs/LootFarmer_Change_81_Final_Boss_Combat_Progression.md`. Catalog writer: `tools/change81_catalog.py` (then `tools/tune_campaign.py`). Probe: `tests/change_81_probe` (maintained suite). Captures: `tests/change_81_capture` → `generated/evidence/change_81/screens/`. Combat ladder harness and CSVs: `tests/tmp/c81/` → `generated/evidence/change_81/` (ignored by Git).

- **Camp Reveal Radius is one global upgrade.** Upgrades → Player → "Camp reveal radius (all camps)": 3 levels for **10 / 20 / 40 Gems** (70 in all, fixed steps), each **+25% of the base camp reveal radius** (additive: +25 / +50 / +75%), for every owned camp on every map, including camps bought later. Permanent (kept by Prestige, cleared by Hard Reset). `campaign.camp_reveal_radius()` = base × (1 + global bonus + any `camp_reveal` bonus), each counted once. No per-camp purchase exists any more: World → Map upgrades shows a one-line note with the shared bonus and the camp panel shows that camp's actual radius. The player (map exploration) Reveal Radius is unchanged.
  - Migration `camp_radius_global` (once): the old per-camp Coin records (`camp_radius:<stage×10+camp>`, up to 4 levels) are removed and the profile gets **global level = its best camp's level, at most 3**. No refund and no new charge; every other camp gains that level. Malformed or zero records grant nothing.
- **Auto Skill needs its skill slot.** Automatic slot N can be bought only when manual Skill Slot N is owned (slot 1 is the default). Enforced in `upgrade_block_reason` for `skill_auto_slots` and every legacy `skill_auto_*` id (`AUTO_SKILL_NEEDS_SLOT`, "Unlock Skill Slot N first", no Gems taken). Prices unchanged (100 / 250 / 500). Capacity bought before this rule is kept; capacity beyond the owned skill slots stays inactive and works once the slot is bought.
- **Spring I / Spring II enemies hit ×1.5.** Every direct hit of a Spring I/II enemy is ×1.50 its Change 80 value; attack intervals are unchanged.
  - Normal types (all four loadouts): `progression.combat.normal_damage_scale` 0.60 → **0.90**.
  - Bosses: damage ×1.5006 (rounded up) through `boss_tuning`, solved against the spot floor, which also reads the scale. HP **×2/3** (each hit is ×1.5, and a full-length fight now deals about the same total damage as before). Without the HP change the first clears moved 1–2 ladder rungs later, and the Green Alien became a wall below P3.
  - Summoned helpers: Sunflower's Sprouts `damage_scale` 0.30 → 0.45 and Green Alien's Green Caps 0.35 → 0.525. The Grim Reaper (Autumn II) is unchanged.
  - Enemy damage skills (Fire, Frost, Lightning, Strike, final-boss attacks) are `definition damage × skill power`, so they rise ×1.5 with the hit and are not multiplied again. Heal (8% max HP; final boss 4%), Focus (+15%), status durations and skill powers are unchanged. Stages 1–6 are unchanged.

## Core loop and controls

Joystick movement, then 0.5 seconds idle before target acquisition/approach/attack. Keep current target until dead or player movement; chain nearby eligible targets. Manual target drag uses line/arrow feedback. Camera drag/pinch/zoom slider; joystick returns camera to player. Manual movement cancels auto-farming focus. Focus chooses enemy type and variant and can return after death. Player HP is red; level bar blue/white. Player starts at level 1; existing level ceiling 1000 remains the baseline.

Death costs time, not possessions. Respawn at selected owned camp. Out of combat (five seconds without being hit, no aggressive enemy) the player recovers 1% max HP per second anywhere and 10% per second beside an owned camp of the current stage; nothing recovers in combat, so Heal and potions keep their roles. Purchasing a camp does not silently select it. Combat effects/rewards are simulation-owned once per action; animation/audio never apply a second hit. Panels do not pause combat; explicit global pause freezes simulation. Settings/panel navigation remains usable while paused. Cancel pending windups on load. Hard Reset clears progression, including keys/cards, and retains settings.

Use Kenney Fantasy UI and supplied PixelPlains content. Keep visual composition inspector-editable where robust. Long panels scroll with pinned actions, safe-area layout and proper touch arbitration. Do not restore retired Cartoon GUI styling. Remove permanent overhead variant-description text only; names/HP remain. Tutorial may explain variants. Recent finds remain top-left (last three, expandable to ten; no coin spam).

## Enemies, variants and loot

Normal types have outfit-only, weapon-only and both variants. Selected types additionally have a fourth extra-strong variant carrying weapon, outfit and skill. It uses the skill and can drop all three through independent rolls. It shares upgrades/achievements with its type and supports focused farming. It remains a normal enemy, with up to six actors per spot from spawn upgrades. First skill variant uses Heal visibly on itself.

Normal skill chance starts at 5%, upgradeable by +15 percentage points. Early bosses use one introduced skill, later bosses two, final boss three complementary skills with clear cues. Boss skill chance starts at 10%, upgradeable to 25%; one skill roll per kill, then choose one of that boss's used skills. No three independent boss skill rolls. Skill drops are base-tier and grow by merging copies of the same skill (Change 8; no skill evolution). **Gear (Change 7):** weapons are shared families — Sword and Axe, each a 7-tier material ladder (Wood, Stone, Iron, Gold, Diamond, Emerald, Obsidian) with its own owned icon and swing — so a Wood Sword is one identity whichever enemy carries or drops it. Outfits stay tied to their enemy's look. Each boss carries and drops the weapon one tier above its stage's normal enemies, in its own family (Snowman: Stone Axe; Spring II bosses: Emerald Sword since Change 9); enemy damage stays authored by tools/tune_campaign.py, so the weapon is not counted twice. Drops are rolled per individual enemy death. Currency credits immediately; ground items retain source type. No manual selling or auto-sell remains in final design.

## Inventory, merges, evolution and copy bonuses

Shared inventory holds weapons, outfits and nonconsumable skills; consumables have separate capacity. One slot per distinct owned tier identity; duplicates stack without a designed gameplay quantity cap, including when capacity is full. Maximum purchasable capacities derive from the complete catalog including evolutions. Capacity is checked before acquiring a new identity or evolving into a missing identity. One equipped weapon/outfit. Each distinct owned UNEQUIPPED weapon contributes 30% of its stats once, and each unequipped outfit 15% (Change 9), not once per duplicate. The equipped item counts once at full value. Outfit milestones count only for the worn outfit. Weapons stay at 30%: they are 14 shared identities and the only damage scaling (Change 9 Notes). Stored skills confer no passive stats.

Merge cap ^100 for weapons; ^500 for outfits and skills (Change 8), which grow only from copies of themselves. Outfits (Change 9): core = base × (1 + 0.02·min(m,100) + 0.01·max(0,m−100)), so ^100 3×, ^300 5×, ^500 7×. The worn outfit also gains milestones scaled by its tier t: ^100 Guard 3t Defense, ^200 +10% outfit HP, ^300 Recovery 0.05%·t max HP/s, ^400 Guard 6t, ^500 Guard 10t and Recovery 0.08%·t. Skills keep the Change 8 curve (effective levels = 100 + 100·ln(1 + (m−100)/100)). Coefficients live in catalog.progression.merge and outfit_milestones. Same-id duplicates power merges below cap. Merge and random-roll records survive zero stock during the run. At ^100, current spare quantity gives a small bonus; copies are NOT consumed merely to gain this bonus and no reservation bucket exists. Spending/using copies reduces their corresponding bonus. Bonus is quantity-dependent, not a permanent incremental reward. Random rolls do not grow with merges or stock bonuses.

Evolution (weapons only since Change 11; supplies grow by mastery, see Change 11) needs ^100 and at least 101 source copies: consumes 100, retains one source copy and its progression, and grants a successor. New successors start ^0; existing successor progression persists. Successor intrinsic ^0 base stats equal 85% of source ^100 base stats, excluding stock bonuses, random rolls and external player/card buffs. Later merges surpass the predecessor. Same rule for weapons and consumables; tier 7 remains the current authored ceiling. Outfit and skill tiers that no enemy drops remain only so older saves keep evolved copies (catalog `legacy`). Same-art successors use distinct stable ids and tier colors. Source stock bonuses decrease when evolution spends copies. Final-tier stock can still grow. Items and all their progression reset on prestige.
